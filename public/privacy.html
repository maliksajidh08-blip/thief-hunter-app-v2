Privacy Policy & Data Security Standards

Last Updated: September 2026. Your privacy and hardware security are the absolute core of our mission.

1. Privacy-First Architecture

Thief Hunter Guard operates under a strict principle of Zero-Knowledge Local Processing. Sensor evaluation (accelerometer, gyroscope, proximity) and AI intruder face detection run entirely on-device using Android native hardware APIs and Google ML Kit. Your biometric features and face pictures never leave your device without explicit emergency trigger configuration.

2. What Information We Access and Why

  • Camera (Intruder Selfie): Accessed strictly upon detected unauthorized touch, incorrect unlock attempts, or alarm triggers. Automatically closes after 5 seconds to prevent camera hardware heating and conserve power. Photos are saved in private app storage.
  • Location (GPS & Network): Queried during active tracking or upon receipt of verified emergency SMS commands. In Ultra Low Power Mode, location polling is halted completely until an emergency theft state is confirmed.
  • SMS Permissions: Used solely to transmit encrypted emergency distress coordinates to your pre-authorized family contacts and receive emergency remote commands (LOCATE, SIREN, LOCK).
  • Battery & Thermal Telemetry: Monitored locally to prevent device battery overheating and gracefully throttle heavy tasks when temperature exceeds 40°C.

3. Data Retention & Automatic Cleanup

To preserve mobile storage on devices with limited internal flash memory (such as 32GB or 64GB phones), our built-in database optimizer automatically purges location breadcrumb trails older than 24 hours and caps the intruder photo cache at 20 snapshots.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal location data, device identifiers, or biometric snapshots with advertisers, data brokers, or commercial marketing entities.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You maintain full sovereignty over your data. You may wipe the local intruder vault, clear historical GPS breadcrumbs, or reset device pairing at any moment directly from the in-app Settings menu.

6. Contact the Data Protection Officer

For privacy inquiries, audit requests, or compliance questions, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@thiefhunterguard.app.
